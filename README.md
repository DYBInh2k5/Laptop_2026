## What This Repository Covers
- Recommendations by major (IT, Design, Business, Engineering, Healthcare, and more)
- Budget tiers from entry-level to premium
- Hardware spec baselines (CPU, RAM, SSD, GPU, display, battery)
- Buying strategy, deal timing, and scam warning checklist
- Setup, optimization, maintenance, and troubleshooting guidance

## Target Audience
- University and college students
- Parents buying laptops for students
- Academic advisors and student support teams

## Suggested Usage
1. Open the major section in [Computer_Recommendations_By_Major.md](Computer_Recommendations_By_Major.md).
2. Match your budget tier.
3. Compare at least 2 model options.
4. Read setup and maintenance sections before purchase.

## Notes
- Prices are indicative and vary by location and promotions.
- Model availability changes frequently.
- Always verify warranty and return policies.
