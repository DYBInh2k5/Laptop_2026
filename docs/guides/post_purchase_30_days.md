# Ke Hoach 30 Ngay Sau Khi Mua

Huong dan 30 ngay de may on dinh, an toan, va san sang cho hoc tap dai han.

## Ngay 1-3: Setup Nen Tang
- Cai he dieu hanh sach (neu can)
- Cap nhat driver va firmware
- Cai bo phan mem hoc tap co ban
- Bat xac thuc 2 lop cho tai khoan quan trong

## Ngay 4-7: Kiem Thu Co Ban
- Test pin trong 1 ngay hoc thu
- Test nhiet do khi dung software chinh
- Kiem tra ket noi Wi-Fi, camera, micro
- Tao restore point hoac snapshot

## Tuan 2: Toi Uu Nhe
- Giam startup app
- Sap xep thu muc du an
- Bat auto backup cloud
- Dat lich update hang thang

## Tuan 3: On Dinh Workflow
- Tao template ghi chu/bao cao
- Tao folder mau theo mon hoc
- Dong bo tai lieu tren 2 thiet bi neu co

## Tuan 4: Bao Tri Dau Tien
- Kiem tra dung luong SSD
- Kiem tra app nao hao RAM/CPU
- Danh gia co can them phu kien khong

## Ket Qua Mong Doi
- May vao trang thai on dinh
- Khong loi vat va trong hoc ky dau
- Co backup va quy trinh cap nhat ro rang
