# Vietnam Tier-1 Cities Pricing

Reference for major urban markets such as Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi.

## Typical Channel Types
- Official retail chains
- Brand-authorized stores
- High-volume marketplaces with verified sellers

## Price Behavior
- Better stock on mainstream models
- Frequent campaign discounts and card promotions
- Accessories often bundled in student seasons

## Indicative Delta by Segment
| Segment | Typical Delta vs Base MSRP |
|---|---:|
| Entry office | -5% to +5% |
| Mid productivity | -8% to +3% |
| Premium ultrabook | -5% to +8% |
| Gaming/performance | -10% to +5% |

## Buying Notes
- Verify exact SKU and RAM/SSD config.
- Ask for final invoice value after all vouchers.
- Confirm warranty is manufacturer-backed.
