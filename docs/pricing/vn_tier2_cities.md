# Vietnam Tier-2 Cities Pricing

Reference for secondary city markets and smaller local channels.

## Typical Channel Types
- Local electronics stores
- Regional distributors
- Marketplace orders with intercity shipping

## Price Behavior
- Selection is narrower than tier-1 cities
- Delivery time can be longer for custom configs
- Promotions may be less frequent but negotiable

## Indicative Delta by Segment
| Segment | Typical Delta vs Tier-1 |
|---|---:|
| Entry office | +0% to +6% |
| Mid productivity | +2% to +8% |
| Premium ultrabook | +3% to +10% |
| Gaming/performance | +2% to +9% |

## Buying Notes
- Ask for sealed-box verification before payment.
- Clarify return window and shipping liability.
- Check after-sales service location in your city.
