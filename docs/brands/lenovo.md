# Lenovo

## Strengths
- Strong keyboard quality in ThinkPad/ThinkBook
- Good value-to-performance ratio
- Wide range across student and enterprise segments
- Usually easy upgrade options in many models

## Trade-offs
- Product naming can be confusing
- Build quality varies significantly by lineup
- Touchpad quality can be average on lower tiers

## Best-fit Users
- Students typing/programming for long sessions
- Engineering and technical majors
- Buyers who want upgrade flexibility

## Typical Budget Bands
- IdeaPad: budget to mid
- ThinkBook/ThinkPad: mid to premium
- Legion: gaming/performance range

## Buying Notes
- Prioritize ThinkBook/ThinkPad for consistency.
- Verify display panel type before checkout.
