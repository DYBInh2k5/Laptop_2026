# HP

## Strengths
- Large availability across online and retail channels
- Good mainstream options for office and study
- Premium design lines in Spectre/Envy

## Trade-offs
- Panel quality can vary by SKU
- Battery and thermals differ significantly by lineup
- Need careful model-by-model checking

## Best-fit Users
- Students wanting broad local availability
- Productivity users in budget and mid segments

## Typical Budget Bands
- Pavilion: budget to mid
- Envy/Spectre: mid to premium

## Buying Notes
- Always verify exact panel and battery specs.
- Check return policy in case of screen quality mismatch.
