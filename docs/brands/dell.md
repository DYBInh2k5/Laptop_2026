# Dell

## Strengths
- Balanced product lines from entry to premium
- Good Linux/Windows compatibility in many models
- Strong business support options
- Often good keyboard and chassis quality in XPS/Latitude

## Trade-offs
- Premium lines can be expensive
- Some consumer lines vary by region and batch
- Port selection can be limited on ultrabooks

## Best-fit Users
- Students wanting stable Windows workflow
- Business and productivity users
- Engineering students needing scalable options

## Typical Budget Bands
- Inspiron/Vostro: entry to mid
- XPS/Latitude/Precision: mid to premium

## Buying Notes
- Compare service policy by seller.
- Check thermal profile on H-series models.
