# MSI

## Strengths
- Strong GPU-focused performance options
- Good high-refresh panels in gaming categories
- Suitable for heavy compute and rendering tasks

## Trade-offs
- Battery life is often below ultrabook class
- Can run hot/noisy under sustained load
- Heavier form factors in high-performance models

## Best-fit Users
- Students with gaming + AI/ML + rendering workloads
- Users who prioritize raw performance over portability

## Typical Budget Bands
- Mid gaming: mid-tier budgets
- High-end gaming/creator: premium budgets

## Buying Notes
- Test sustained load thermals before final purchase.
- Factor charger size and travel weight into decisions.
