# Apple (MacBook)

## Strengths
- Excellent battery life and low standby drain
- Stable performance on battery
- Strong display quality and speakers
- High resale value

## Trade-offs
- High upfront pricing
- Limited upgrade path after purchase
- Fewer ports on lighter models
- CUDA-based workflows are not native

## Best-fit Users
- Students prioritizing mobility and battery
- Creative users using Final Cut/Logic/Adobe workflows
- Users wanting long-term build quality

## Typical Budget Bands
- Air line: mid-tier student budgets
- Pro line: high-tier creator and engineering budgets

## Buying Notes
- Configure RAM and SSD correctly at purchase time.
- Verify software compatibility for your major first.
