# ASUS

## Strengths
- Strong OLED offerings in multiple tiers
- Competitive pricing in performance categories
- Broad portfolio: Vivobook, Zenbook, TUF, ROG, ProArt
- Good options for students needing GPU acceleration

## Trade-offs
- Software utilities can be inconsistent
- Service experience may vary by region
- Thermal/noise profile differs heavily between models

## Best-fit Users
- Design and content students
- IT students needing value GPU options
- Buyers balancing display quality and budget

## Typical Budget Bands
- Vivobook: budget to mid
- Zenbook/ProArt: mid to premium
- TUF/ROG: gaming/performance tiers

## Buying Notes
- Check color coverage if buying for design.
- Check fan noise and sustained performance.
