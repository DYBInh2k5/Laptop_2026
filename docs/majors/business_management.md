# Nhom Nganh Kinh Te, Quan Tri, Dich Vu

Tai lieu nay tach rieng cac nganh co workflow van phong, phan tich du lieu, va giao tiep khach hang.

## Nganh Bao Gom
- Quan tri va Marketing
- Kinh te va Quan ly
- Tai chinh va Ngan hang
- Du lich va Khach san

## Cau Hinh Nen Co Theo Muc Do

### Muc co ban (Office + hoc tap)
- CPU: Intel Core i5 U-series hoac Ryzen 5 U-series
- RAM: 8GB (uu tien nang cap 16GB)
- SSD: 512GB
- GPU: iGPU
- Man hinh: 14 inch FHD IPS
- Pin: 8-12 gio

### Muc trung binh (Excel nang, BI, da nhiem)
- CPU: Intel Core i7 hoac Ryzen 7
- RAM: 16GB
- SSD: 512GB-1TB
- GPU: iGPU hoac GPU co ban
- Man hinh: 14-16 inch, 2K neu co
- Pin: 8+ gio

### Muc nang (financial modeling + dashboard + script)
- CPU: Intel Core i7 H-series hoac Ryzen 7 H-series
- RAM: 32GB
- SSD: 1TB
- GPU: RTX 4050/4060 neu co machine learning tai chinh
- Man hinh: 15-16 inch, do sang cao

## Bo Phan Mem De Xuat
- Office: Excel, PowerPoint, Word
- Visualization: Power BI, Tableau
- Finance: Python (pandas), R, Stata/EViews
- Quan ly cong viec: Notion, Trello
- Hop truc tuyen: Zoom, Teams, Meet

## Goi Y Chon Nhanh Theo Nhu Cau
- Di hoc nhieu, can pin: MacBook Air M2/M3, Dell XPS 13
- Tap trung gia hop ly: ASUS Vivobook 14/15, Lenovo IdeaPad
- Lam tai chinh/chung khoan: Lenovo ThinkBook 16, Dell XPS 15

## Sai Lam Thuong Gap
- Chon laptop gaming nang chi de dung Office
- Mua 8GB RAM nhung mo 20 tab + Excel file lon
- Bo qua chat luong ban phim (anh huong hoc va viet bao cao)

## Kich Ban Mua Hang De Xuat
1. Chon 2 model cung tam gia.
2. So sanh pin, keyboard, man hinh truoc.
3. Kiem tra bao hanh va chinh sach doi tra.
4. Chot model co 16GB RAM hoac nang cap duoc.
