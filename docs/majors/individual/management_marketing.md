# Quan Tri va Marketing

- CPU: Intel Core i5/Ryzen 5 tro len
- RAM: 16GB uu tien
- SSD: 512GB
- GPU: iGPU la du
- Man hinh: 14-15.6 inch FHD IPS
- Pin: 8-12 gio

- Notion, Trello, Slack
- Zoom, Google Meet

## Goi y theo ngan sach

- 10-15M: Dell XPS 13, MacBook Air M2
- 18M+: MacBook Pro 14, Dell XPS 15
