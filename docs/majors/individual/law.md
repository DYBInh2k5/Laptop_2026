# Luat Hoc

## Muc tieu cau hinh
- CPU: Intel Core i5/Ryzen 5
- RAM: 8-16GB
- SSD: 512GB
- GPU: khong bat buoc
- Pin: 8-12 gio

## Phan mem thuong dung
- Office, PDF annotation
- Cong cu quan ly tai lieu
- Legal database client tren web

## Goi y theo ngan sach

- 12-15M: MacBook Air M2, Dell XPS 13
