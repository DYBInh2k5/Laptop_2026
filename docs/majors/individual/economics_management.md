# Kinh Te va Quan Ly

## Muc tieu cau hinh
- CPU: Intel Core i5/Ryzen 5 tro len
- RAM: 8-16GB
- SSD: 512GB
- GPU: iGPU
- Man hinh: 14-15.6 inch
- Pin: 8+ gio

## Phan mem thuong dung
- Excel nang, PowerPoint
- Power BI co ban
- Browser da tab

## Goi y theo ngan sach
- 7-9M: ASUS Vivobook 14
- 12-15M: MacBook Air M2, Dell XPS 13
- 18M+: Dell XPS 15, MacBook Pro 14

## Checklist chot mua
1. Ban phim va touchpad em.
2. Pin va sac nhanh on.
3. Webcam/micro chat luong.
4. Bao hanh va doi tra ro rang.
