# Nhom Nganh Cong Nghe va Ky Thuat

Tai lieu nay uu tien performance cho lap trinh, mo phong, CAD, AI/ML, va workload render.

## Nganh Bao Gom
- Cong nghe thong tin
- Vien thong va CNTT lien quan
- Ky thuat (xay dung, co khi, dien)

## Cau Hinh Theo Kich Ban

### Lap trinh co ban (web/app)
- CPU: i5/Ryzen 5 tro len
- RAM: 16GB
- SSD: 512GB
- GPU: iGPU
- Man hinh: 14-15.6 inch FHD/QHD

### AI/ML, data engineering
- CPU: i7/Ryzen 7 tro len
- RAM: 32GB
- SSD: 1TB
- GPU: RTX 4060 tro len (uu tien CUDA)
- Man hinh: 15-16 inch, tan so quet cao neu code nhieu

### CAD/Simulation/3D
- CPU: i7/i9 H-series
- RAM: 32GB
- SSD: 1TB
- GPU: RTX 4060/4070 hoac workstation class
- Man hinh: 16 inch QHD, mau on dinh

## Phan Mem Theo Nhom
- Dev: VS Code, JetBrains, Git, Docker
- AI/ML: Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, CUDA toolkit
- CAD: AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Revit
- Network: Packet Tracer, Wireshark, GNS3

## Chien Luoc Ngan Sach
- 8-12M: coding co ban, can uu tien RAM va SSD
- 12-18M: can bang coding + AI nhe + CAD co ban
- 18-30M: AI/ML va CAD nang, render du an lon

## Danh Gia Nhanh Truoc Khi Mua
- Co dual-channel RAM khong
- SSD co con slot mo rong khong
- TGP cua GPU la bao nhieu
- Tan nhiet co on khi load lien tuc 20-30 phut khong

## Ghi Chu Su Dung Thuc Te
- Neu hoc AI nghiem tuc: uu tien RTX 4060 tro len
- Neu hoc backend/web thuong: i5 + 16GB la du
- Neu hoc CAD ky thuat: man hinh 16 inch giup quan sat ban ve ro hon
