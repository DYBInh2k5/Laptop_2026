# Nhom Nganh Sang Tao, Truyen Thong, Giao Duc Nghe Thuat

Tai lieu nay tap trung vao man hinh, mau sac, GPU, va workflow content creation.

## Nganh Bao Gom
- Thiet ke do hoa
- Bao chi va Truyen thong
- Nghe thuat va Su pham

## Baseline Cau Hinh

### Design 2D co ban
- CPU: i5/Ryzen 5
- RAM: 16GB
- SSD: 512GB
- GPU: RTX 4050 hoac iGPU manh
- Display: IPS/OLED, uu tien 100% sRGB

### Video editing va motion
- CPU: i7/Ryzen 7 tro len
- RAM: 32GB
- SSD: 1TB
- GPU: RTX 4060 tro len
- Display: 15-16 inch, do phan giai 2K tro len

### Drawing va digital art
- May chinh: laptop IPS/OLED
- Bo sung: iPad + Pencil hoac may 2-in-1 co but
- Uu tien do tre but thap va panel mau chuan

## Software Stack
- Adobe: Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects
- Alternative: DaVinci Resolve, Affinity suite, GIMP, Krita
- Motion/UI: Figma, Blender (co ban), CapCut desktop

## Quy Trinh Chon May Cho Creative
1. Kiem tra panel mau (sRGB/DCI-P3) truoc tien.
2. Chon RAM 16GB toi thieu, 32GB neu video.
3. Chot SSD 1TB neu dung footage lon.
4. Kiem tra he thong tan nhiet va do on khi render.

## Bo Loc Theo Ngan Sach
- 8-12M: hoc thiet ke co ban, chinh sua anh
- 12-20M: da so sinh vien design/video
- 20M+: studio workflow, render nang

## Goi Y Phu Kien
- Man hinh ngoai 27 inch 2K/4K
- O cung SSD ngoai 1TB de luu project
- Chuot va ban phim ngoai de edit lau
