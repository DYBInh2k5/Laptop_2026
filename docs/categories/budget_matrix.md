# Ma Tran Budget Theo Nhu Cau

Tai lieu nay giup chot nhanh muc gia phu hop theo workload thay vi theo thuong hieu.

## Muc Gia De Xuat

| Muc gia | Nhu cau phu hop | Cau hinh toi thieu |
|---|---|---|
| 5-8M | Office, web, hoc online | i5/R5, 8GB RAM, 256-512GB SSD |
| 8-12M | Coding co ban, design nhe | i5/R5, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD |
| 12-18M | Da so sinh vien | i7/R7, 16GB RAM, 512GB-1TB SSD |
| 18-25M | AI/ML, video, CAD trung-nang | i7/R7, 32GB RAM, RTX 4060 |
| 25M+ | Du an nang, workflow chuyen nghiep | i9/R9/M3 Pro+, 32GB+, RTX 4070+ |

## Quy Tac 70-20-10
- 70% ngan sach cho laptop
- 20% cho phu kien quan trong
- 10% du phong bao hanh, nang cap, sua chua

## Muc Tieu Theo Vong Doi 4 Nam
- Nam 1-2: khong nghen RAM/SSD
- Nam 3: chi nang cap SSD neu can
- Nam 4: duy tri on dinh, backup du lieu tot

## Checkpoint Truoc Khi Chot Gia
- Co can GPU roi khong
- Co can pin > 10 gio khong
- Co can man hinh chuan mau khong
- Co nhu cau mang di hoc moi ngay khong
