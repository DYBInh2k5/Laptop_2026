# Ma Tran Chon Loai May

Tai lieu nay tra loi cau hoi: nen chon laptop thuong, gaming, 2-in-1, tablet hay desktop mini.

## So Sanh Nhanh

| Loai may | Di dong | Hieu nang | Pin | Nang cap | Doi tuong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ultrabook | Rat tot | Trung binh-kha | Rat tot | Han che | Sinh vien di hoc nhieu |
| Gaming laptop | Trung binh | Cao | Yeu-trung binh | Kha | CNTT, ky thuat, game |
| 2-in-1 | Tot | Trung binh | Kha | Han che | Ghi chu, ve, presentation |
| Tablet + keyboard | Rat tot | Trung binh | Tot | Khong | Ghi chu, ve, hoc co ban |
| Desktop mini | Kem | Kha-cao | N/A | Tot | Hoc tai nha, setup co dinh |
| Mobile workstation | Trung binh | Rat cao | Trung binh | Kha | CAD, 3D, render |

## Lua Chon Theo Tinh Huong
- Di hoc lien tuc, can nhe: ultrabook
- Hoc AI/ML: gaming laptop hoac workstation
- Ve digital: tablet hoac 2-in-1
- O nha nhieu: desktop mini + man hinh ngoai

## Cac Cau Hoi Chot Loai May
1. Ban mang may di hoc may ngay moi tuan?
2. Ban co can GPU roi de hoc khong?
3. Ban co can but cam ung de ve/ghi chu khong?
4. Ban co san man hinh ngoai tai nha khong?
