# Student Laptop Recommendation Guide

A practical handbook to choose laptops by major, budget, and software workload.

## Highlights
- Multi-major recommendations
- Clear budget segmentation
- Hardware baseline by use case
- Buying and deal strategy
- Setup and troubleshooting checklists

## Main Document
- Full guide: [Computer_Recommendations_By_Major.md](Computer_Recommendations_By_Major.md)

## Typical Decision Flow
1. Identify your major and primary software.
2. Pick a budget range.
3. Choose 2-3 candidate models.
4. Validate RAM/SSD upgradability and warranty.
5. Check setup and maintenance notes before buying.

## Who Should Use This
- Students selecting their first study laptop
- Parents comparing options quickly
- Advisors supporting student purchases

## Disclaimer
Prices and availability can change fast. Always verify local pricing, seller reputation, and warranty terms.
